📖 Conte de Vie

A YouTube channel of short French stories.

This page exists to support contedevie-uploader, a private automation tool used by the channel owner to publish their own short videos to their own YouTube channel on a schedule.

The tool is not a public product. It has no users other than the channel owner, no sign-up, and no interface for anyone else.
